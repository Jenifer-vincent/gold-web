# Gold Forecasting Dashboard

## Smart gold investment analytics — know exactly when your money comes back.



### ❓ Problem Statement:


Millions of Indians invest in physical gold (22K)— but most have no idea when they'll recover their money.
Between making charges (8–20%), GST (3%), wastage fees, and market volatility, the actual break-even point is much later than people expect. There's no simple tool that tells an everyday investor:

"You bought 10g of gold at ₹10,000/g in Jan 2024 — you'll recover your full investment by March 2026."

This dashboard solves exactly that.

### ⚙️ Current System:

Traditional forecasting relies on static charts, manual calculations, and limited visibility into policy, festival, and global market influences. Investors often depend on guesswork or fragmented tools, leading to poor timing decisions.

### ✨ Features:

📊 Break-even Forecaster — Enter purchase details, get a month-by-month profit/loss prediction powered by SARIMA

🧠 Market Intelligence — Policy impact, interest rate sensitivity & festival demand analysis

📈 Economic Analysis — Inflation-adjusted returns & CPI-corrected forecasts

💡 Strategy Planner — Personalized sell/hold advice based on your risk tolerance

🌍 Regional & Global Prices — Compare 22K gold across 12 Indian states & 11 countries

👤 Portfolio Tracker — Track your investment value, P/L and holding period

🔔 Chatbot — Instant answers on trends, resale, economics & model explanations

💱 Currency Converter — Live conversion via Frankfurter API
